device-generator's data, in resources/, comes from these sources.

Derived by the scripts in scripts/ (see doc/data.md for each column):

- IP blocks (ipv4.tsv, ipv6.tsv): the delegated-extended statistics of the
  five regional internet registries, AFRINIC, APNIC, ARIN, LACNIC and
  RIPE NCC.
- Internet users (countries.tsv, internet_share): World Bank, indicator
  IT.NET.USER.ZS, CC BY 4.0.
- IPv6 use (countries.tsv, ipv6_share): APNIC Labs IPv6 measurement.
- Populations (countries.tsv): country-generator, which states its own
  sources.
- Release dates (releases.tsv): endoflife.date (MIT), Chromium Dash and
  Mozilla product-details.

Curated for this library from general knowledge, the vendors' documentation
and rounded public usage statistics (StatCounter and similar); nothing was
copied from a dataset, so they are released under the MIT licence of the
library: platforms, platform shares, maker shares, models, adoption curves,
version shares, browser requirements, browser shares, user-agent templates,
screens, and the Samsung Internet rows of releases.tsv. The documentation
ranges in reserved.tsv are from RFC 5737 and RFC 3849.
